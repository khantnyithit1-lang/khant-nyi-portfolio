Discuss Requirements ↗
Why is the setup fee 0 Ks?

The initial template-based website customization and deployment are included in the agreed subscription scope. Additional custom work is priced separately.

What happens after the first 3 months?

You can discuss renewal or ending the subscription. Domain ownership, website source handover and hosting arrangements should be specified in the signed agreement before work begins.

Does the monthly fee include all hosting costs?

The monthly fee covers the agreed website service, monitoring and updates. Domain registration, any paid hosting, third-party subscriptions and AI usage fees are quoted separately.

Can I request a fully functional CRM or LMS?

Yes, requirements can be discussed for a custom quotation. The current portfolio demos are frontend prototypes, not production-ready backend applications.
