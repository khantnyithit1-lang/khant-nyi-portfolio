A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Khant Nyi Thit.

I'm Khant Nyi Thit, a freelancer passionate about digital marketing, graphic design, website development, and creative technology.

My passion for graphic design inspires me to create engaging visuals, advertising creatives, and meaningful digital experiences.

Alongside my creative work, I have foundational knowledge of Frontend Development, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and responsive web design.

On the Backend Development side, I've gained hands-on experience working with Cloudflare Workers, D1 databases, SQL, APIs, and webhooks through practical projects involving CRM systems, Telegram bots, and AI integrations.

I enjoy exploring Full-Stack Web Development, combining frontend interfaces, backend functionality, databases, and AI automation to build useful digital solutions.

By bringing together creativity, digital marketing, and technology, I aim to help businesses strengthen their online presence and simplify everyday workflows.

Create with purpose. Build with creativity. Make technology useful.
