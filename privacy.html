PRIVACY NOTICE

Respecting Your Information.

A straightforward explanation of how the portfolio contact form is intended to be used.

When you submit a project inquiry, the form collects the name, contact details, selected service, optional package and budget, and project information you choose to provide.

The form is intended to be processed by Netlify Forms after the website is deployed on Netlify. Submissions are used to read and respond to your project inquiry. The website itself does not include a separate database collecting inquiries.

You can contact khantnyithit1@gmail.com to ask about information you submitted. Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive personal information in the project details field.

External links to Telegram, Facebook and project demos open services with their own privacy policies.

Any future analytics, tracking, or additional integrations should be disclosed here before they are enabled.
